How to read the chart

Match your hand to the dealer’s visible card.

Each square gives you the recommended move. Read from your hand on the left across to the dealer’s face-up card at the top.

  1. 1
    Choose your hand type

    Hard: no ace counted as 11. Soft: an ace is counted as 11. Pair: two cards of the same value.

  2. 2
    Find the row and column

    Find your total or cards down the left, then the dealer’s face-up card along the top.

  3. 3
    Play the matching letter

    Where the row and column meet, use H, S, D, or P as explained in the key below.

Example You have a hard 12 and the dealer shows 4. Find row 12 and column 4: S means stand.

H Hit S Stand D Double* P Split
