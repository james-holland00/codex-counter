Privacy policy

Counted is designed to work privately on your device. It does not require an account and does not sell, share or transmit your training data.

Information stored on your device

Counted stores practice progress, session history, achievements, theme choice and sound preference locally on your device. This information is used only to operate the app and personalise your training.

Information we collect

Counted does not collect personal information, analytics, advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, payment information or browsing history. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Network access and third parties

The core app and its fonts are bundled with the app and work offline. Counted contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. If you choose Counted Pro, Apple processes the purchase and subscription through the App Store under Apple’s privacy terms. Counted checks only whether the subscription entitlement is active and does not receive your payment-card details.

Deleting your information

Because training information stays on your device, it can be removed by deleting the app. Browser installations can also remove it by clearing Counted’s site data.

Children and responsible use

Counted is an educational card-counting trainer and does not offer gambling, wagering or prizes. Parents and guardians should decide whether blackjack-related training content is appropriate for a child.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new effective date. Privacy enquiries can be made through the contact method on the Counted support page.
