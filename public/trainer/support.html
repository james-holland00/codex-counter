Help & support

How can we help?

Counted works offline and stores training progress only on the current device.

Sound is not playing

Open Settings from the top-right corner and make sure Sound effects is on. Your iPhone’s volume and Silent Mode settings can also affect playback.

Progress is missing

Progress does not sync between devices. Deleting the app, clearing website data or reinstalling Counted removes locally stored progress.

A training screen appears paused

Flash pauses when the app moves into the background. Casino Mode waits for your decisions between automatic dealer actions, so you can return to the table without losing your place.

Counted Pro purchase or restore

Open Counted Settings, choose Counted Pro and tap Restore purchases. Use Manage subscription there to view or cancel an active subscription through Apple. Counted never receives your payment-card details.

Contact

Email counted.help@outlook.com. We aim to reply to support enquiries as soon as practical.

When reporting a problem, include the Counted version, iPhone model, iOS version and what happened immediately before the issue.
